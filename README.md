🚁 MediDrone AI

Smart Medical Delivery & Emergency Response System

One System. Faster Medical Delivery. Smarter Emergency Response.

📌 Project Overview

MediDrone AI is an AI-assisted smart medical delivery and emergency response prototype designed to improve medical supply delivery planning and emergency coordination.

The system combines an interactive command center, drone operations visualization, emergency response information, AI-assisted logistics, and medical supply monitoring in a unified dashboard.

The project demonstrates how artificial intelligence and drone technology can support healthcare logistics and emergency medical assistance.

🎯 Objectives

- Improve medical delivery planning during emergencies.
- Support emergency response coordination.
- Demonstrate AI-assisted logistics recommendations.
- Visualize drone operations and monitoring information.
- Support medical inventory and cold-chain monitoring.
- Explore the application of AI in healthcare logistics.

✨ Key Features

🚁 1. 3D Command Center

Provides a visual command center for viewing and demonstrating drone operations.

🚨 2. Emergency Response

Displays emergency response information to support medical delivery coordination.

🏥 3. Hospital Network

Represents the hospital network for medical delivery planning and healthcare coordination.

🚁 4. Fleet Operations

Provides a section for viewing and managing drone operation information within the prototype.

🔧 5. Predictive Maintenance & AI Motor Telemetry

Demonstrates drone maintenance and motor telemetry monitoring concepts.

💊 6. Smart Inventory

Represents medical supply inventory information to support delivery planning.

📊 7. AI Demand Forecast

Demonstrates AI-assisted forecasting of medical supply requirements.

🌡️ 8. Cold-Chain Telemetry

Represents temperature monitoring concepts for medical supplies that require controlled storage conditions.

🧠 9. AI Logistics Advisor

Provides AI-assisted recommendations for medical delivery and logistics planning.

📋 10. Compliance Audit

Provides a section for demonstrating compliance and audit information.
